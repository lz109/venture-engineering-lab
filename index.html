Overview

AI-augmented venture development under uncertainty.

VEL turns venture research into tools, courses, and live experiments for HKU's innovation ecosystem.

Evaluate

Assess ventures, patents, and pitch decks with comparable human and AI judgments.

Outputs include evaluation rubrics, score summaries, disagreement maps, and next-step recommendations.

Experiment

Turn uncertainty into hypotheses, field tests, and decision-ready evidence.

Teams define assumptions, success metrics, evidence plans, and learning loops before committing resources.

Educate

Offer capstones and courses where learners build on live lab systems.

Students learn by shipping useful modules for founders, ecosystem partners, and research projects.

Offerings

What VEL offers.

VEL is organized around the people we serve.

For Founders & Researchers

Evaluate ideas, test assumptions, and build evidence for new ventures and technologies.

Venture Evaluation · Patent Assessment · AI Mentorship

Use VEL to sharpen venture claims, assess patent or technology readiness, and convert uncertainty into evidence-building experiments.

For Innovation Partners

Build better tools and systems for managing and evaluating innovation programs.

Ecosystem Metrics · Program Analytics · AI-enabled Infrastructure

Work with VEL on dashboards, program-running infrastructure, evaluation workflows, and policy analytics for innovation ecosystems.

For Students

Learn venture engineering through hands-on projects, research, and real-world challenges.

Capstone Studio · Research Projects · Open Courses

Join through capstones, research projects, and courses that connect AI-native venture methods with live lab systems.
